Exclusive Itinerary

Our 3D2N Komodo flow,

Pre-Trip · Sept 15, 2027
Fly into Labuan Bajo, check in to a hip local hotel, then warm up the weekend with nightlife and rooftop views at Le Pirate or Seaesta.

Board Sora, settle in over gourmet lunch, then Kelor Island trekking, Manjarite freediving test session, and Kalong Island sunset bat spectacle.

Day 3 · Sept 18
Morning at Kanawa Island coral gardens, scenic sail back to harbor, then around 1 PM airport drop-off to fly back to KL.
