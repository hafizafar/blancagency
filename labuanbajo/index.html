What Awaits You

5 Reasons Your Friends
Will Never Forgive You for Missing This

Komodo in September is a different world — crystal visibility, calm seas, zero crowds, and nature performing at its absolute peak.

🤿 Reason 01

Freedive with Giant Manta Rays at Manta Point

September brings peak underwater visibility — up to 30m of clarity. Manta Point delivers schools of oceanic mantas in their natural habitat. No cages, no barriers. Just you, the ocean, and the most graceful creature on Earth.

🏖️ Reason 02

Pink Beach — One of Earth's Seven Pink Sand Beaches

A surreal stretch of rose-tinted sand coloured by crushed red coral. Swim in water so clear you can count the fish below your feet. Private access straight from the liveaboard.

🌅 Reason 03

Sunrise Trek on Padar Island — That View

The legendary three-bay panorama at first light. A 20-minute hike rewards you with one of Southeast Asia's most photographed landscapes — with just your group and nobody else.

🐋 Reason 04

Witness the September Whale Migration Through the Deep Channels

September is the secret window — blue whales and sperm whales pass through Komodo's deep straits on their annual migration. You'll watch from the bow of your private yacht as they surface and breach. Zero crowds. Pure magic.

🦎 Reason 05

Face to Face with Komodo Dragons

The world's largest living lizard — up to 3 metres long. A ranger-guided trek through Rinca Island to see them up close. Naturally terrifying. Absolutely unmissable.

Night One — Before You Board

Land First.
Labuan Bajo Rooftop Vibes.

Before you set sail, spend your first night living it up on land. Labuan Bajo has become one of Indonesia's hottest emerging destinations — a string of rooftop bars and sunset restaurants perched above a glittering harbour where the drinks are cold and the sky puts on a show every evening. Consider it the opening act.

  • Sunset dinner overlooking the harbour marina
  • Rooftop cocktail bars with panoramic bay views
  • Fresh local seafood from the night market
  • Boutique hotels and guesthouses steps from the dock
